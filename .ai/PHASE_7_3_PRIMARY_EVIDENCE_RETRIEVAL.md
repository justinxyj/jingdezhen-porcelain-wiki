## Evidence types used
- archaeological excavation records / reports
- digitized historical primary texts
- museum collection records
- library/archive records
- official government plans / gazetteers

- scholarly works treated as primary sources for the history of the scholarship itself

## Important boundary
Primary Evidence Retrieval is complete. **Evidence synthesis is not.**

A mapped primary source does not automatically make every claim in an Entry true. The next QA layer must map individual claims to the retrieved evidence and distinguish:
1. directly observed/documented fact;
2. source author's interpretation;
3. later scholarly interpretation;
4. unresolved scholarly dispute.
