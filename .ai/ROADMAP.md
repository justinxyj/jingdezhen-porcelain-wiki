# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
MkDocs museum shell, knowledge navigation, Supabase layer, GitHub Pages.

## Phase 1 — Knowledge-layer stabilization
Durable AI memory; verify public-read/RLS; stabilize /entry/; media provenance/correctness; schema/migration/frontend alignment; CI/Pages verification; repeatable branch/PR/review workflow.

## Phase 2 — Content depth
Source-backed history, objects, kiln sites, people/institutions, 72-step craft process, trade/exchange, terminology and aesthetics.

## Phase 3 — Digital museum experience
Geographic entrance, timeline exploration, object storytelling, process visualization, relationship browsing, responsive mobile experience.

## Phase 4 — Knowledge graph and discovery
Relationships, faceted search, related content, provenance/source exploration, graph views where useful.

## Phase 5 — Intelligent assistance
Source-grounded retrieval/Q&A with citations and editorial safeguards.

## Phase 6 — Release hardening
Regression, performance/mobile, accessibility/SEO, provenance audit, deployment verification.
